1. Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract. One account per person. Accounts are personal and cannot be sold, shared or transferred. We may ask for identity verification before paying out, and may close accounts that fail it.

2. Points, value and expiry

Points are a loyalty unit, not money, and are not a security or deposit. The published conversion is 1,000 points = $1.00. We will not reduce the conversion rate for points already in your ledger. Points stay valid for 12 months after your last activity; balances below the cash-out minimum cannot be redeemed for cash.

3. Offers, verification and reversals

Offers are supplied by third-party advertising networks. Their confirmation is final: points are credited only on a verified completion, and are reversed if the advertiser later rejects the conversion. Reversals appear in your ledger with the network's stated reason, and you may appeal once through support.

We do not pay for viewing or clicking ordinary display advertising. Paid activities are surveys, installed-app actions, signups and sponsor clips delivered through offer partners that expressly permit incentivised completion.

4. Cash-out policy

✓Minimum cash-out: 5,000 points ($5.00). No withdrawal fee.
✓Methods: PayPal or bank transfer. Payouts are made to an account in your own name.
✓Runs are processed weekly; most transfers arrive within 1–2 business days after processing.
✓Identity and tax details may be required once lifetime earnings reach the reporting threshold in your jurisdiction (for example $600 in the United States).
✕No deposits, top-ups or "activation" payments are ever required to earn or withdraw.

5. Referral programme

The programme is one level deep. A fixed bonus (currently 500 points) is paid once, after a referred member's first verified offer completion. There is no commission on a referral's future earnings, no multi-level structure, and a cap of 50 paid referrals per account. Bonuses are funded from marketing spend and are not a condition of anyone's payout.

6. Earnings disclosure

Earnings depend on the offers available in your country and profile. Survey inventory is finite. A realistic range for an active member is a few dollars to a few tens of dollars per month; screenshots of large weekly earnings are not typical and should not be treated as a promise. Nothing on this site is financial, tax or investment advice, and no outcome is guaranteed.

7. Privacy summary

We collect what paying and preventing fraud requires: account handle, contact details, payout details, offer completion events, and technical signals (IP, device and browser fingerprint, approximate location). We share completion data with offer partners and payout data with payment providers. We do not sell personal data. You can request an export or deletion at any time, subject to records we must keep for tax and anti-fraud purposes.

This prototype stores a demo profile in your browser's local storage only; nothing is transmitted to a server.

8. Prohibited conduct

No multiple accounts, VPN or proxy masking, emulators or automation, fake or purchased identities, incentivising third parties to complete offers on your behalf, or attempting to reverse-engineer verification. Violations forfeit the balance and close the account.

9. Contact

Support: support@taskmint.example (placeholder). Registered entity, address and company number to be inserted before launch. Complaints are acknowledged within two business days.
