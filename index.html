What we never do
✕Charge deposits, "activation" fees or VIP packages.
✕Pay for clicking or viewing ordinary display ads.
✕Run multi-level referrals or team commissions.
✕Pay only in USDT, on one manual monthly date.

Why not pay per ad view?

Major web ad networks forbid rewarding people for viewing or clicking ads — and the honest payout for a single display impression is fractions of a cent. Offers work because the advertiser pays for a completed action.

Why is the code one level deep?

Referral bonuses come out of our marketing budget and stop there. A site that funds payouts from recruitment has to keep recruiting to survive — that is the pattern behind most of these sites collapsing.

How fraud is handled

One account per person, device fingerprinting, proxy/VPN checks and offerwall rejection reconciliation. Rejected offers are reversed in your ledger with the partner's reason attached.

Questions

The ones people actually ask

Can I really cash out $5?

Yes — 5,000 points. There is no withdrawal fee. The prototype in this repo records requests and debits points; live payments require the backend described in the README.

Do I need to spend money?

No. Signup offers never require a card, and anything that does is excluded from the catalogue. Deposits are not part of the model.

How much can I realistically earn?

Survey inventory limits everyone: most members complete a handful of offers a week. $5–$40 a month is a realistic range, not a salary — be suspicious of anything promising more.

What happens if an offer is rejected?

The advertiser's postback is authoritative. Rejections appear in your ledger with the partner's reason, and you can appeal once through support.
