Methodology

What each number means

Members paid

Unique accounts with at least one completed payout that month. Repeat payouts count once.

Median time

Median hours between request and confirmed receipt, not our internal queue time. Averages hide outliers — median is the honest one.

Rejected

Payouts cancelled for fraud checks or duplicate identities, as a share of requests. Every rejection comes with a written reason.

How to verify a rewards site before joining

✓Payout history with counts, not screenshots of single transfers.
✓A company entity, address and contact route that is not only Telegram.
✓Cash out to PayPal or bank — crypto-only payouts have no chargeback pressure.
✓No deposits, no "activation" fees, no VIP packages, no team commissions.
✕One payout date per month, decided by the operator, is a red flag — not a feature.
